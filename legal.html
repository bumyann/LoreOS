Terms of Service § 1

Who we are

LoreOS is a creator's sanctuary — an all-in-one creative workspace for AI roleplay creators. Lorebook editor, character card editor, chat completion preset editor, and a private journal, all in one place. No accounts, no subscriptions, nothing leaves your browser unless you choose to sync it. Just your work, organized.

Built and maintained by an independent developer. Made with AI assistance by someone who writes a lot of lore and has poor impulse control.

Privacy Policy § 2
LoreOS does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on any server. Everything you create lives in your own browser or your own Google Drive account.

Data stored locally

All lorebooks, character cards, presets, journal pages, and settings are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it or enable Google Drive sync.

What We Collect § 3

Nothing

  • No account registration or login required
  • No analytics, tracking scripts, or telemetry
  • No cookies beyond what your browser sets locally
  • No server-side storage of any kind
  • No advertising, ad networks, or data brokers

Third-party services

LoreOS loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google's own privacy policy applies to that request. No other third-party services are loaded unless you choose to enable Google Drive sync.

Google Drive Sync § 4

Google Drive sync is entirely optional. If you choose to connect it:

  • LoreOS requests the drive.appdata scope only — a hidden app folder not visible in your Drive, inaccessible to any of your existing files
  • Your data is written directly from your browser to your own Google Drive — it does not pass through any LoreOS server
  • Your Google account credentials are never seen or stored by LoreOS — authentication is handled entirely by Google's OAuth system
  • Access tokens are stored in memory only and cleared when you close or refresh the page
  • You can revoke LoreOS's Drive access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions

Google's use of information received from LoreOS is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
